The Institutional Framework

Georgia Tech Digital Media Handbook

After comps, LMC students identify their Dissertation Chair and two committee members. The oral defense evaluates the candidate's original and intended contributions to the field. Passing the defense secures the committee signatures on the Dissertation Topic Approval Form. Admission to Ph.D. candidacy requires completing coursework, passing comps, and filing the Request for Admission to Candidacy Form.

Dissertation Risk Mapping

Scope protections
Risk Meaning Prevention / Mitigation
Concept sprawl Too many brilliant terms. Keep only three: operative description, worldtext, thick prompting.
Archive sprawl Too many projects/case studies. Choose exactly 2–3 cases; appendix the rest.
Committee confusion Faculty cannot place the project. Give each member a role: theory, method, artifact, ethics.
Method weakness Thick prompting sounds like vibes. Define evidence units, logging protocol, selection criteria.
Artifact overwhelm The making eclipses the argument. Every artifact must directly prove the contribution statement.
Proposal delay No firm chair/committee timeline. Schedule chair meeting in June; committee asks in August.
Writing avoidance Endless mapping without prose. Minimum 5 pages/week beginning in July.

Dissertation Defense & Citations

Preparing to defend the contribution architecture

Scholarly justification and literature backing for every node in Watson Hartsoe's dissertation framework.

1. Enters Field (Disciplinary Home)

  • ✓
    Digital Media & Media Studies: Generative AI is a new class of world-forming media. We extend Mitchell’s concept of the imagetext (the friction and merging of visual/verbal representations) into the computational worldtext.
    [Cite: Mitchell, W. J. T. (1994). Picture Theory. University of Chicago Press.]
  • ✓
    HCI & Practice-Based Design: Creative prompting is an agential design practice, not a mechanical routine. We treat it as steering within high-dimensional vector distributions.
    [Cite: Zimmerman, Forlizzi, & Evenson (2007). Research through Design in HCI. CHI '07; Gaver, B. (2012). Annotated Portfolios. interactions.]
  • ✓
    Narrative & Cybernetic Loops: Human-AI storytelling is a cybernetic feedback loop governed by statistical viscosity.
    [Cite: Aarseth, E. (1997). Cybertext: Perspectives on Ergodic Literature. JHU Press; Wiener, N. (1948). Cybernetics.]

2. Responds to Limit (The Scholarly Gap)

  • ✕
    Confronts: Prompting treated as mere technique. We show prompting is a cultural, narrative, and epistemic practice.
    [Cite: Oppenlaender, J. (2024). Prompt Craft as Socio-Technical Art. ACM Comput. Surv.]
  • ✕
    Confronts: AI outputs evaluated as isolated content. We show outputs converge to a default "platform mean" (twelve lighthouses) if not dynamically steered by human agency.
    [Cite: Hintze, Proschinger Åström, & Schossau (2026). Autonomous Loops and Convergent Lighthouses.]
  • ✕
    Confronts: Authorship debates stuck on human vs. machine. We shift the binary to distributed agential action. The prompter acts as a cybernetic pilot navigating a statistical medium.
    [Cite: Suchman, L. (1987, 2007). Plans and Situated Actions. Cambridge University Press.]
  • ✕
    Confronts: AI ethics reduced to bias, hallucination, and consent. We expand ethics to examine the politics of the generated world itself—what is stabilized, silenced, or defaulted.
    [Cite: Bajohr, H. (2024). Platform Realism and the Ethics of Latent Space; Spivak, G. C. (1988). Can the Subaltern Speak?]
  • ✕
    Confronts: Media theory lacking vocabulary for executable description. Modern ekphrasis theory (Spitzer, Heffernan) narrowed ekphrasis to passive artwork description. We recover the ancient production-oriented function.
    [Cite: Webb, R. (1999, 2009). Ekphrasis, Imagination and Persuasion in Ancient Rhetorical Theory. Ashgate.]

3. Intervenes Through Method (The Theoretical Wedge)

  • ✦
    Operative Description & Ekphrasis: Description is the control signal that generates visual space in multi-modal manifold environments.
    [Cite: Bajohr, H. (2024). Operative Ekphrasis. Digital Rhetoric Journal.]
  • ✦
    Thick Prompting: Anthropological thick description adapted to document the agential loops of creative prompting across six distinct context layers.
    [Cite: Geertz, C. (1973). The Interpretation of Cultures. Basic Books.]
  • ✦
    Worldtext Narrative Systems: Spatial systems wrapping text-model generation into coherent, persistent environments.
    [Cite: Wolf, M. J. P. (2012). Building Imaginary Worlds. Routledge; Aarseth (1997). Cybertext.]
