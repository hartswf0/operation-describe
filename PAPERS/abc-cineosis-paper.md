## 1. Thesis & Core Research Problem

### The Refined Contribution Sentence
This project investigates whether the concept of **operative description**—studied through the method of **thick prompting**—can isolate a distinct class of descriptive acts that govern the generation, revision, and traversal of moving-image **worldtexts** (recursive, state-bound simulation loops).

Rather than declaring that "language operates systems," this dissertation acts as a research engine to test a specific suspicion: **Under what conditions does the description/action pair alter the probability distribution of subsequent generative states, and when does it collapse into decorative redundancy?**

The core research question is:
> **What difference does a description make when its output is not a sentence or a still image, but duration, motion, and cinematic return?**

We formulate the test metric as the **generation-space delta ($\Delta G$)**:

$$\Delta G = G_{\text{description}} - G_{\text{default}}$$

*   **No $\Delta G$ (Decorative):** Changing description $D$ produces no measurable difference in output, revision path, or operator action.
*   **Weak $\Delta G$ (Interpretive):** Changes the viewer's interpretation but does not alter generative trajectory.
*   **Medium $\Delta G$ (Generative):** Changes the output style, camera motion, or spatial layout.
*   **Strong $\Delta G$ (Structural):** Alters the revision path, constraint limits, or world-state continuity across loops.
*   **Full $\Delta G$ (Systemic):** Modifies the entire human-machine loop: prompt, computational mediation, moving-image output, and the return path that shapes the next prompt.

## 2. Lineage Interrogation: The Uncertain Pedigree

Instead of using theoretical ancestors as a shielding canon to prove our thesis, we interrogate each thinker. What do they allow us to ask, what do they forbid us from overclaiming, what would they criticize, and what difference must make a difference for them to matter?

### 1. Ludwig Wittgenstein (Philosophical Investigations)
*   **Allows us to ask:** What kind of language-game is generative prompting? How does a prompt act as a rule that compiles a temporary game for the model?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** We cannot claim that a model "understands" the meaning of a prompt. Meaning is not an internal state of the neural weights; it is only the public, rule-bound use of the token inside the generation loop.
*   **Criticism of the project:** Wittgenstein would warn that we are creating a private language of "operative description" and falling into grammatical confusion by treating mathematical probabilities as "rules."
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** If a change in the prompt's logical grammar does not alter the boundaries of what is a "legal move" in the generated scene, then the prompt is not acting as a rule.

### 2. J. L. Austin (How to Do Things with Words)
*   **Allows us to ask:** What kind of performative doing occurs when a prompt produces media rather than an immediate social act?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** Prompts are not sovereign speech acts. They do not instantly alter social institutions or guarantee illocutionary success; they are hostage to the model's priors and the platform's processing stack.
*   **Criticism of the project:** Austin would note that prompting lacks the shared, conventional social context that makes performatives work. An LLM cannot make a promise or marry a couple; it only mimics the locutionary surface.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** The prompt is only performative if its locutionary input forces a perlocutionary rasterization of pixels that the operator cannot bypass.

### 3. Clifford Geertz (The Interpretation of Cultures)
*   **Allows us to ask:** What changes when ethnographic thick description is converted into thick prompting? How does layering cultural codes prevent latent drift?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** A prompt cannot be "thick" in the sense of capturing lived human experience. It only simulates thickness by packaging keywords into structured data.
*   **Criticism of the project:** Geertz would criticize the attempt to reduce rich, interpretative anthropology to a predictable prompt template or engineering checklist.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** Thick prompting is only a method if a third-party researcher, using the 6-layer rubric, can reproduce the same style constraints and get similar outputs across different models.

### 4. Geoffrey Bowker & Susan Leigh Star (Sorting Things Out)
*   **Allows us to ask:** When does a prompt phrase or classification label cease to be expressive and become infrastructural?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** A single user prompt is not infrastructure. It is only the transient signal; the infrastructure resides in the platform queues, training datasets, and safety filters.
*   **Criticism of the project:** They would argue that our focus on individual prompts hides the massive, invisible systems of labor and classification that actually route platform governance.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** A prompt becomes infrastructural only when changing its category routes the file to a different execution queue or server pipeline.

### 5. Lucy Suchman (Plans and Situated Actions)
*   **Allows us to ask:** How do prompt-output-revision loops repair failed descriptions when they crash against the model's training priors?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** We cannot write a prompt that acts as a master plan. The generation is always situated, and control is an illusion repaired through retries.
*   **Criticism of the project:** Suchman would criticize any framework that treats "steering" as a deterministic control system rather than an ad-hoc, situated negotiation.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** The loop is only situated if the operator's next revision is a direct reaction to a breakdown or failure in the previous output.

### 6. Edwin Hutchins (Cognition in the Wild)
*   **Allows us to ask:** Where is interpretation located: in the user's mind, the model weights, the interface code, the archive logs, or the loop itself?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** The human is not the sole author. Cognition and meaning are distributed across the entire computational stack.
*   **Criticism of the project:** Hutchins would argue that treating the description/action pair as the unit of analysis isolates the sign from the physical environment and interface habits.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** Cognition is only distributed if changing the interface layout (e.g. `c2.html` panels) changes how the operator revises their descriptions.

### 7. Harun Farocki (Operational Images)
*   **Allows us to ask:** What is the descriptive equivalent of an operational image—a description that does not represent but acts as an element of an operation?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** Most prompts remain decorative or consumer-oriented; they do not participate in industrial or military operations.
*   **Criticism of the project:** Farocki would warn that converting creative writing into operational instructions capitulates to the militarized, technical rationalization of the image.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** A description is operational only when its failure halts the execution of a larger cyber-physical system.

### 8. Tarleton Gillespie & Sarah T. Roberts (Platform Governance)
*   **Allows us to ask:** What forms of governance and labor asymmetries enter when generated worlds are steered through automated thresholds?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** We cannot claim that steering is democratic or free when it is bounded by proprietary platform API boundaries and safety moderation layers.
*   **Criticism of the project:** They would point out that studying prompting as an aesthetic performance erases the precarious, low-wage human labor that filters the training data.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** Platform governance matters only if changing the classification threshold (e.g. "toxic" flag) alters the financial cost or visibility of the output.

### 9. David Deamer (A Deleuzian Cineosis)
*   **Allows us to ask:** What changes when *cineosis* (cinematic semiosis) is no longer a camera-world-image relation, but a prompt-system-motion-return loop?
*   **Forbids us from overclaiming:** Generative video models do not create time-images or movement-images in the Deleuzian sense; they interpolate pixels based on statistical patterns. They lack duration (*durée*).
*   **Criticism of the project:** Deamer would argue that Peirce’s and Deleuze’s semiosis requires an encounter with a real, temporal world, which a latent distribution cannot provide.
*   **Difference that must make a difference:** Deleuzian cineosis is only relevant if the generated camera movement and frame duration alter the operator's subjective interpretation in a way that static frames cannot.

## 4. Bounded Case Studies & Verification Plan

To verify these concepts under pressure, we execute two comparative experiments:

### Case 1: The Primary Case (LLM Tool Schemas in `c2.html`)
*   **The Test:** Hold the user prompt and the base LLM constant. Vary only the `description` field within the JSON tool schema (e.g. `"Issue refund"` vs. `"Issue refund only if invoice is uploaded"`).
*   **Verification:** Measure the delta in invocation rates and the propagation of parameter checks.
*   **Failure State:** If the model invokes the tool at identical rates regardless of semantic differences in description, the claim of *operative description* is falsified.

### Case 2: The Moving-Image Case (ABC Cineosis in `c2.html` BEFLIX)
*   **The Test:** Generate a dot-matrix animation sequence using `c2.html`. Run two loops: one where the user only prompts for static scenes (Thin) and one where the prompt describes motion, camera transitions, and durations (Thick).
*   **Verification:** Trace the **encounter trace** $\langle A, B, C, R, A' \rangle$.
*   **Failure State:** If the transition from still image to moving image does not introduce distinct failure modes (e.g. temporal collapse, motion artifacts) that alter the operator's revision path, *ABC Cineosis* is demoted to a decorative subcase.
