_extracted_text — plain-text conversions of the .docx in this directory
======================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
  One .txt per .docx, produced with `textutil -convert txt`. A format
  conversion, not an interpretation: no editing, no selection, no summary.
  Filenames preserve the originals exactly, including the $ and _ prefix
  variants, because those variants are distinct artifacts — several are
  near-duplicate renditions of the same argument and the duplication is
  itself evidence about how the corpus was drafted.

WHY IT EXISTS
  The .docx are binary and cannot be read or quote-checked in a browser.
  Cards in the SLIPCASE derivative field cite this corpus by title and
  section. Without a text rendering those citations are unverifiable by
  anyone but the author on their own machine.

  Specifically, these cards depend on it:
    FORAGE-DX-001  the herbarium / Ogilvie      (The Worldtext and the Weed)
    FORAGE-DX-005  observer declaration          (Second-Order Thick Prompting)
    FORAGE-DX-006  the two compilers / LDraw     (Words That Make Things)
    FORAGE-DX-008  Wunderkammer / Linnaean       (The New Atlantis of Latent Space)
  and FORAGE-DX-002, -003, -007 quote it as well.

  Derivative field: https://github.com/hartswf0/slipcase
  Reader:           https://hartswf0.github.io/slipcase/   (SLP / FIELD 008)

CAVEAT ON THE SOURCES THEMSELVES
  These documents are AI-generated deep-research reports. They cite Ogilvie,
  Louvel, Hasson, Spivak and others with bare superscripts and no page
  numbers. Every card foraged from them marks those attributions [UNVERIFIED].
  Treat the .txt as a faithful rendering of the .docx — not as a warrant for
  what the .docx claims.
